_**Executive Summary**_

This project establishes a comprehensive **Corporate Growth Architecture and Capital Allocation Strategy** for **PT Bina Auto Solusi (BINASOL)**, a multi-industry holding company overseeing three distinct business units:
1. **Beauty & Healthcare (Primary Growth Engine):** Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) skincare and health products with a high contribution margin (>56%).
2. **Film & Creative Media (Content & CAC Catalyst):** Digital media production serving as an in-house marketing engine to lower customer acquisition costs.
3. **Trading & Construction (Stability Anchor):** Industrial construction and material supplier securing baseline cash flow through stable B2B contracts.

The strategic objective was to resolve capital allocation inefficiencies across capital-intensive B2B segments and transition the holding toward a highly scalable, high-margin D2C ecosystem.


_**Business Problem & Core Challenge**_

* **Margin Volatility in Legacy Lines:** The Construction and Film divisions required capital investments that scaled linearly but suffered from unpredictable revenue cycles and client-dependent margins.
* **Scalability Bottlenecks:** The Beauty & Healthcare division demonstrated rapid cash turnover and high contribution margins but faced infrastructure and technology constraints.
* **Capital Misallocation Risk:** Strategic dilution across underperforming business units threatened holding-level return on capital.


_**Key Business & Financial Insights (EDA & Projections)**_

* **Operating Leverage Acceleration:** Consolidated Net Profit is projected to grow at a **50.86% CAGR** (from Rp702M in 2025 to Rp2.41B in 2028), far outpacing Total Revenue CAGR (**22.85%**, reaching Rp8.90B). This proves massive cost-efficiency driven by contract manufacturing (maklon) economies of scale.
* **Revenue Share Drift:** The Beauty & Healthcare segment increases its revenue contribution from **52.1% (2025) to 65.2% (2028)**, becoming the dominant profitability driver.
* **Capital Risk Resilience (MoS Elasticity):** Net Profit Margin (NPM) expands from **14.6% to 27.1%**. Every 1% increase in NPM correlates with a **1.9% expansion in the Margin of Safety (MoS)**, pushing holding-level loss tolerance to **57.9% by 2028**.
* **CAC Efficiency via Synergy:** Leveraging the Film & Creative Media unit for organic in-house product placement reduces reliance on paid advertising, driving a projected **35% reduction in Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** and boosting LTV:CAC ratio to **5.2x** by 2028.


_**Strategic Recommendations & Implementation Roadmap**_

1. **Prioritized Initial CAPEX Allocation (Rp340 Million in 2025):**
   * **Rp150M:** R&D for a white-label 3-in-1 family health screening device.
   * **Rp60M:** Omnichannel e-commerce and POS system integration.
   * **Rp130M:** Equipment upgrades for Film and Construction units.
2. **Asset-Light Execution (Contract Manufacturing):**
   * Utilize contract manufacturing (maklon) for skincare and supplements to minimize fixed factory infrastructure costs while protecting gross margin target at **60.0% by 2028**.
3. **Cross-Unit Ecosystem Integration:**
   * Reallocate Rp200M in tied-up liquidity from high-risk construction tenders to supply working capital for the Beauty segment's faster cash turnover cycle.


_**Frameworks & Analytics Methodology**_

* **RICE Scoring Model:** Ranked Beauty & Healthcare as **#1 (RICE Score: 28,500)** vs. Construction (**RICE Score: 39**), analytically validating 100% initial CAPEX focus on the Beauty line.
* **Break-Even & Sensitivity Analysis:** Stress-tested model against CAC surges (+20%) and revenue drops (-10%), proving the holding operates safely above BEP (Rp3.75B minimum revenue required in 2028).
* **Ansoff Matrix & Business Model Canvas (BMC):** Designed a recurring revenue ecosystem combining main hardware devices (*The Hook*) with recurring consumables (*Serum & Soothing Gel*).
