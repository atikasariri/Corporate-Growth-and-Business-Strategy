# **Corporate Growth & Business Strategy**

_**Executive Summary**_

This repository contains a comprehensive strategic business analysis and financial growth model developed for a multi-industry holding company. The project focuses on optimizing capital allocation, leveraging EDA, and applying the RICE prioritization framework to drive long-term profitability.


_**Project Overview & Key Highlights**_
* **Problem Statement:** Stagnant holding growth (1% YoY) with sub-optimal capital distribution across high-risk business units.
* **Strategic Solution:** Reallocated capital focus toward the **Beauty & Healthcare** division as the primary growth engine, supported by a D2C expansion model.
* **Financial Impact:** Projected an increase in Net Profit Margin (NPM) from **14.6% to 27.1%** and expanded the Margin of Safety to **57.9%** by 2028.


_**Key Frameworks & Analytics Used**_
* **Financial Modeling & Projection:** Evaluated CAGR, NPM, and valuation margins.
* **Prioritization Matrix:** Applied **RICE Scoring** to rank business unit potential.
* **Market & Business Strategy:** Ansoff Matrix, Competitor Analysis, and D2C Go-To-Market strategy.
